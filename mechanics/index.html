Mechanics Laboratory Practicals

The five laboratory practicals follow the current USTHB Faculty of Physics L1 Mechanics sequence: rectilinear motion, circular motion, Newton's second law, friction/contact force, and energy transfer.

5 core practicalsUSTHB sequencemeasurement → graph → dynamics
PRACTICAL 1 · M01 · REVIEWED

Rectilinear Motion

Animated light-gate apparatus, repeated timing, manual graph reading, tangent/secant velocity verification, acceleration, motion phases and equations of motion.

Circular Motion

Hammer-bell recording apparatus, calibrated graphical vector work, instantaneous-velocity check, ΔV/acceleration construction, intrinsic components and V(t) comparison tests.

Projectile Dynamics — Newton's Second Law

Scale-1/80 trajectory analysis, manual Δr/ΔV/acceleration work, P₂ curvature construction, Newton-II force triangles and student-derived air-drag law.

Open practical → PRACTICAL 4 · M04 · REVIEWED

Friction & Contact Force

Animated cart/elastic/hammer recording, manual 0.08 s strip measurements, official V₁/V₂ graph scales, contact-force decomposition, elastic tension and mass-independence test.

Open practical → PRACTICAL 5 · M05 · REVIEWED

Energy Transfer

Current inclined two-body USTHB setup with suppressed early ticker marks, manual 4/50 s processing, S₁/S₂ friction, spring-force diagrams and complete Ec/Epg/Epe/ET analysis.

Energy Transfer

Study a two-body system on an inclined plane with two contact surfaces and a spring; connect kinematics, dynamics, friction and the evolution of mechanical energy.

USTHB core workflowmeasurement → graph → dynamicsbrowser-local progress

Official apparatus

Body A: MA = 510 g on a plane inclined at α = 60°. Body B: MB = 580 g, connected by an inextensible string over a pulley. A spring of stiffness k = 9 N/m acts only after it becomes stretched.

The hammer bell records impacts every 1/50 s; the analysis interval is 4/50 s = 0.08 s.

Required sequence

  • Construct V(t) and identify t₀, t₁ and t₂.
  • Determine accelerations in the first two phases.
  • Use Newton's law to calculate μd on S₁ and S₂.
  • Calculate kinetic, gravitational and elastic potential energies.
  • Plot total mechanical energy ET(x).
  • Interpret its decrease as work of non-conservative forces.

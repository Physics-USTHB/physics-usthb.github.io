Circular Motion

Graphical study of a circular trajectory recorded by a hammer bell at 0.01 s intervals: displacement, velocity, acceleration and intrinsic components.

USTHB core workflowLearn / measure / analysebrowser-local progress

Official recording method

A bell mounted in a smooth metal disk moves on an inclined board while tied by an inextensible string to point O. The hammer marks carbon paper every 0.01 s.

The handout asks for an analysis interval Δtₑ = 0.08 s.

V̄ᵢ = Δrᵢ / Δtₑ    and    aᵢ = ΔVᵢ / Δtₑ

Required constructions

  • Successive displacement vectors.
  • Average velocity vectors and instantaneous-velocity approximation.
  • ΔV and acceleration at P₂, P₃, P₄ and P₅.
  • Tangential and normal acceleration components.
  • V(t), then compare at=dV/dt and an=V²/R.

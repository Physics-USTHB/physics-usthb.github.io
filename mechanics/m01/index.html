MECHANICS · L1 SM/ST · LABORATORY PRACTICAL 1

Rectilinear Motion

Experimental kinematics of a cart on an inclined–horizontal rail using two light gates and a digital chronometer: repeated timing, position graph, instantaneous velocity, acceleration, motion phases and equations of motion.

USTHB handout workflowapparatus → measurement → graph → interpretationbrowser-local progress

Objective and experimental system

Study the rectilinear motion of a cart released from rest against stop A on an inclined rail AB joined to a horizontal rail BC. Determine position, velocity and acceleration as functions of time, identify the motion phases, then establish the equations of motion.

s = d + s₀,   s₀ = 2 cm

Light gate B1 remains fixed at s₀. Light gate B2 is moved successively to d = 5, 10, 20, …, 100 cm. Three timing measurements are required at every position.

What you must do yourself

  1. Set the chronometer to L1 start / L2 stop and position B2 correctly.
  2. Release the cart three times at every prescribed distance, resetting after every run.
  3. Construct and read the space diagram s(t−t₀).
  4. Verify that a 0.2 s secant is a valid approximation to the tangent velocity at one point.
  5. Read s from the graph every 0.2 s and calculate the velocity data.
  6. Determine t₀, V₀, s₀, both accelerations, the nature of both phases and the equations of motion.
For d ≥ 50 cm the real handout requires removing the B2 rail support and placing the gate support on the table under the horizontal rail. This handling step is reproduced below.

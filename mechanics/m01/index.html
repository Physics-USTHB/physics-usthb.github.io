Rectilinear Motion

Kinematics of a cart using two light gates and a digital chronometer: repeated timings, s(t), V(t), acceleration and equations of motion.

USTHB core workflowLearn / measure / analysebrowser-local progress

Official objective

Study the rectilinear motion of a cart on an inclined rail joined to a horizontal rail. Determine position, velocity and acceleration as functions of time and establish the equations of motion.

s = d + s₀,   s₀ = 2 cm

Barrier B1 remains fixed. Barrier B2 is placed successively at d = 5, 10, 20, …, 100 cm. For every position, release the cart three times and calculate the mean time.

Real apparatus logic reproduced

  • Cart released from rest against stop A.
  • B1 fixed at s₀ = 2 cm.
  • B2 moved to each prescribed distance.
  • Chronometer must be reset after each timing.
  • Three timings are required at every B2 position.

Dynamics — Newton's Second Law

Official objective

An object of mass m = 50 g is launched in the vertical xOy plane. Its successive positions are given every 0.1 s at scale 1/80. Air resistance is not neglected.

P⃗ + f⃗ = m a⃗

Use Δtₑ = 0.6 s to construct displacement, velocity change, acceleration and drag-force vectors.

Required conclusions

  • Acceleration vectors at P₁…P₄.
  • Normal acceleration and radius of curvature at P₂.
  • Graphical construction f⃗ = m a⃗ − P⃗.
  • Plot |f| as a function of |V|.
  • Infer the form of the drag law.

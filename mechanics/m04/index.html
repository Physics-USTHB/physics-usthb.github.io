Friction & Contact Force

Determine the dynamic-friction coefficient from ticker-tape kinematics and Newton's second law, then test whether it depends on cart mass.

USTHB core workflowmeasurement → graph → dynamicsbrowser-local progress

Official apparatus

A cart of mass M = 1.6 kg is attached to an elastic, released on a horizontal table, and its motion is recorded by a hammer bell every 0.01 s. The hammer itself exerts an approximately constant resisting force f = 0.3 N.

The study interval is Δtₑ = 0.08 s. A second run adds a 1.0 kg mass to the cart.

Required reasoning

  • Construct V₁(t) from successive 0.08 s displacements.
  • Identify the motion phases and instant tₑ when the elastic becomes slack.
  • Determine final-phase acceleration.
  • Resolve contact force C into normal and tangential components.
  • Calculate μd₁ and μd₂ and compare them.
  • Find the elastic tension when speed is maximum.

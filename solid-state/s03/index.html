Theoretical background

Charge carriers moving through a magnetic field experience the Lorentz force q v×B. In a Hall specimen, carriers accumulate on one side until the transverse electric force balances the magnetic force. This produces the Hall field and a measurable transverse Hall voltage.

For a uniform specimen, VH=RHIB/t. The sign of RH identifies the dominant carrier sign, while n=1/(|qRH|) estimates carrier concentration in the single-carrier model. Combining RH with conductivity gives the mobility μ=|RH|σ.

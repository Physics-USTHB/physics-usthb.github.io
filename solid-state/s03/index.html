Hall effect

A current-carrying semiconductor plate in a transverse magnetic field develops a Hall voltage. Reversing I or B reverses the Hall polarity.

VH=RH I B / t

Derived quantities

n=1/(|qRH|),   μ=|RH|σ

The sign of RH identifies the dominant carrier sign in the single-carrier approximation.

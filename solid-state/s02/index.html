Official TP topic: isotropic elasticity

The USTHB programme specifies a torsion pendulum. The reconstruction combines a static torque–angle calibration with free torsional oscillations.

τ=Cφ,   T=2π√(I/C)

Wire elasticity

For a circular wire of length L and radius r, the torsional stiffness is C=πGr⁴/(2L), allowing the shear modulus G to be determined.

Static torsion calibration

Apply several small torques within the elastic regime and record the equilibrium angle.

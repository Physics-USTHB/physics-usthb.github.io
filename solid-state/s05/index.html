Semiconductor conduction

The USTHB programme combines semiconductor conduction with a photovoltaic-cell I–V study. A detailed public equipment handout is not exposed, so this reconstruction uses a hidden intrinsic-like specimen with known geometry.

σ=IL/(VA)
lnσ = const − Eg/(2kT)

Specimen geometry: L=10.0 mm, cross-sectional area A=4.00 mm².

Solar-cell metrics

P=VI
FF=Pmax/(VocIsc)
η=Pmax/(G Acell)

Cell illuminated area Acell=0.0100 m². Record the I–V curve manually, including short circuit, the knee and the open-circuit region.

Temperature-dependent conduction

At a fixed bias, increase temperature stepwise, wait for equilibrium and calculate conductivity from the measured current and specimen geometry.

Theoretical background

Thermionic emission occurs when thermal energy allows electrons to escape a metal surface. In the emission-limited regime the saturation current density follows the Richardson–Dushman law J=ART²e−Φ/(kT), where Φ is the work function.

At lower anode voltage the emitted electron cloud creates space charge that limits the current. For an ideal planar diode this regime follows the Child–Langmuir scaling I∝V3/2. A Richardson plot of ln(J/T²) versus 1/T has slope −Φ/k, so temperature-dependent saturation data can be used to infer the work function.

Two forced coupled oscillators

The same two-loop circuit is now driven sinusoidally. Both capacitor voltages are measured. Each response has two resonances, and the first degree of freedom has an antiresonance between them.

K = (ω₂²−ω₁²)/(ω₂²+ω₁²)

USTHB measurement sequence

  1. Observe resonances and antiresonance at low frequency.
  2. Record V01 and V02 from 1 to 15 kHz, concentrating points around the extrema.
  3. Extract ω₁, ω₂, ωmin and K.
  4. Change C₀, repeat f₁/f₂, and plot ω₁² and ω₂² versus K.

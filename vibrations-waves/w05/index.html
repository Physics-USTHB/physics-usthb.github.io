Loudspeaker in free field

The source loudspeaker is driven through R′=50 Ω. CH2 observes v(t); CH1 observes v(t)+R′i(t). The oscilloscope MATH subtraction gives R′i(t), allowing |Zhp|=V/I.

Loaded tube experiment

After finding free-field resonance f₀, keep frequency fixed. Put the source and identical receiver in the tube, move the receiver to find phase/opposition positions, determine sound speed, then choose quadrature for the loaded-impedance measurement.

Loudspeaker / impedance tube

Start in free field and scan approximately 100 Hz–10 kHz to find the impedance resonance.

Two Coupled Pendulums

USTHB mechanical coupled-mode and beat experiment using two pendulums and a movable spring coupling.

USTHB 2022 TP Ondesmeasurement-first interactionreport-ready data

USTHB pendulum system

Two identical simple pendulums of mass M and length L are coupled by a spring attached at distance a from the pivots. The spring position is varied from 10 to 80 cm.

ω₁²=ω₀²(1−K),   ω₂²=ω₀²(1+K)

Required practical work

  • Remove the spring and measure ~50 periods of one simple pendulum.
  • For at least ten values of a, measure T₁ (in-phase) and T₂ (opposition).
  • Calculate K and plot T₁,T₂ versus K.
  • For five other a values, excite one pendulum only and measure beat and oscillation periods.

Two Coupled Pendulums

USTHB mechanical normal-mode and beat experiment using two pendulums and a movable spring coupling.

simple-pendulum baselinenormal modesbeatscoupling coefficient

USTHB pendulum system

Two identical simple pendulums of length L = 1.00 m are coupled by a spring attached at distance a from the pivots. The spring position is varied from 10 to 80 cm.

ω₁²=g/L,    ω₂²=g/L+2κ(a)    (symmetric spring coupling)

For the in-phase mode the two attachment points move together, so the coupling spring is essentially unextended and the frequency remains that of the uncoupled pendulum. The anti-phase mode stretches/compresses the spring and has the higher frequency.

Required practical work

  • Remove the spring and time about 50 periods of one pendulum.
  • For at least ten values of a, time T₁ (in phase) and T₂ (opposition) and calculate K.
  • Plot T₁ and T₂ versus K.
  • For at least five coupling positions, excite only one pendulum and measure both the fast oscillation period and the beat-envelope period.

Coupled-pendulum timing bench

Start with the spring removed. Place the timing cursors 50 complete periods apart and calculate T=Δt/50.

Koenig trombone / Quincke tube

Two acoustic waves of the same frequency travel over different path lengths and interfere at the receiver. Moving the sliding U-tube changes the path difference by 2Δd.

v = λf,   path difference = 2Δd

USTHB measurements

  1. Fix f between 4.5 and 5.5 kHz; vary Δd from 0 to ~15 cm in 0.5 cm steps and plot yR(Δd).
  2. Deduce the period in Δd and the speed of sound.
  3. Then vary f from 1 to 6 kHz and measure D between successive amplitude maxima.
  4. Plot D versus 1/f and obtain v with uncertainty.

Interference tube

For the first part, keep frequency constant near 5 kHz while scanning Δd.

Electrical analogue of a damped oscillator

USTHB uses a series RLC circuit instead of a mechanical mass–spring–damper. The capacitor voltage VC(t) plays the role of displacement x(t). The excitation is a square signal of amplitude 0.4 V and frequency ≤100 Hz.

δ = RT/(2L),   ω₀ = 1/√(LC)

Core tasks

  1. Increase R from zero until the critical regime is obtained; determine RC and L.
  2. Return R to zero and measure pseudo-period Ta and logarithmic decrement D.
  3. Deduce δ, Q, L and residual resistance Rr.
  4. Replace R and C by unknown Rx, Cx and determine them from D′ and T′a.

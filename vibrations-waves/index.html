VOM — Vibrations & Waves

The suite follows the USTHB Faculty programme and its Boudjema–Bourdache practical manuals: an oscilloscope/GBF introduction, four oscillation TPs, and six wave manipulations.

Oscillations

Electrical analogues of mechanical vibration are used to study damping, resonance, coupled modes and antiresonance.

TP00 · INTROlive

Oscilloscope & GBF Introduction

Dual-trace oscilloscope, calibrated scales, triggering, function generator, graphing and phase measurement.

Open TP00 → TP01 · 1DL FREElive

Free Oscillations — 1 Degree of Freedom

Damped RLC analogue, critical regime, logarithmic decrement, pseudo-period, quality factor and unknown R/C.

Open TP01 → TP02 · 1DF FORCEDlive

Forced Oscillations — 1 Degree of Freedom

Amplitude resonance, phase lag, bandwidth, quality factor and resonance-based unknown R/C measurement.

Open TP02 → TP03 · 2DL FREElive

Free Oscillations — 2 Degrees of Freedom

Coupled symmetric RLC oscillators, beats, normal modes, proper frequencies and coupling coefficient.

Open TP03 → TP04 · 2DF FORCEDlive

Forced Oscillations — 2 Degrees of Freedom

Two resonances, antiresonance, V1/V2 response curves and influence of capacitive coupling.

Waves

Six manipulations from the USTHB 2022 TP Ondes polycopy, from mechanical coupling to acoustic and coaxial-wave propagation.

ONDES Ilive

Two Coupled Pendulums

Normal modes, beats, coupling position, period measurement and coupling coefficient.

Open manipulation I → ONDES IIlive

Koenig Trombone / Quincke Tube

Acoustic interference, path difference, maxima spacing and the speed of sound in air.

Open manipulation II → ONDES IIIlive

Ultrasound in Fluids & Solids

Propagation time, longitudinal/transverse speeds, elastic properties and water-temperature dependence.

Open manipulation III → ONDES IVlive

Vibrating String

Detector alignment, fundamental frequency, tension, length, linear density and log–log exponents.

Open manipulation IV → ONDES Vlive

Loudspeaker & Sound in Air

Electromechanical input impedance, resonance, tube propagation, phase and sound speed.

Open manipulation V → ONDES VIlive

Waves Along a Coaxial Line

Incident/reflected pulses, reflection coefficient, attenuation, characteristic impedance and standing-wave modes.

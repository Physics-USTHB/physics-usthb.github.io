Symmetric coupled system

USTHB uses two identical RLC loops coupled by a capacitor C₀. With a low-frequency square excitation, each half-period can be treated as a free response.

K = C/(C+C₀),   Ω₁²=ω²(1−K),   Ω₂²=ω²(1+K)

What must be observed

  • Beats when only one oscillator is initially excited.
  • In-phase normal mode from q₁+q₂.
  • Opposition normal mode from q₁−q₂.
  • T₁, T₂ and K.

Waves Along a Coaxial Line

USTHB 200 m transmission-line experiment in pulse and sinusoidal regimes.

USTHB 2022 TP Ondesmeasurement-first interactionreport-ready data

USTHB coaxial line

The line consists of two 100 m cable coils AB and CD, joined at midpoint BC. The practical studies pulse propagation first, then sinusoidal propagation and standing-wave behaviour.

Γ=(ZT−Zc)/(ZT+Zc)

Core sequence

  • Identify incident and reflected pulses; determine propagation speed.
  • Vary terminal resistance and measure |Γ|; compare with theory.
  • Measure attenuation at 0,100,200,300,400 m effective travel.
  • With sine excitation, compare input/output delay with matched load.
  • Study short/open terminations; infer λ(f) and propagation speed.

USTHB introduction session

This session reviews the dual-trace oscilloscope and low-frequency function generator before the vibration experiments. The manual explicitly requires amplitude, period/frequency, phase, triggering, XY composition and calibrated scales.

Required operating habits

  • VOLT/DIV and TIME/DIV must be calibrated.
  • Choose AC/DC/GND coupling deliberately.
  • Synchronise the trace with a valid trigger source and level.
  • Use two channels for phase measurements.
  • Use XY only when composition of two signals is intended.

Theoretical background

A sinusoidal voltage is described by v(t)=V₀sin(2πft+φ). The oscilloscope converts voltage into vertical deflection and time into horizontal deflection, so amplitude, period and frequency are obtained from calibrated divisions. For two equal-frequency signals, a time shift Δt corresponds to Δφ=2πΔt/T.

Triggering fixes a repeatable phase point of the waveform to the screen and therefore produces a stable trace. In XY mode the time base is replaced by one channel driving X and the other driving Y; two sinusoids then produce a Lissajous figure whose shape reflects their frequency ratio and phase difference.

Eleven 1 Ω sections in series between A and C; the selector connected to B provides RAB=0…11 Ω.

Analog scale reading

If the needle indicates n divisions on a scale of N divisions, with selected range CAL, then

X = (n/N) · CAL.

The handout uses a 0–30 scale with the 0.3, 3, 30 and 300 ranges, and a 0–100 scale with the 1, 10 and 100 ranges.

Rule: start on the maximum range, then step down until the needle deflection is as large as possible without exceeding full scale.

Uncertainty used in the TP

ΔXclass = CAL · class/100
ΔXread = CAL · Δn/N
ΔX = ΔXclass + ΔXread

This is the worst-case absolute-uncertainty convention taught in the handout. The simulation uses meter class 1.5 and a reading estimate Δn = 0.5 division.

Safety and laboratory discipline

  1. Every new circuit must be checked before the source is connected.
  2. Measurement instruments must be placed on their maximum range before a new measurement sequence.
  3. Never connect the generator between A and B of the resistance box; the low resistance can produce excessive current.
  4. Before changing the circuit, disconnect the source first.
  5. At the end of the session, switch instruments off and leave the bench ready for the next group.

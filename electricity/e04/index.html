What the oscilloscope measures

In sweep mode, horizontal position represents time and vertical deflection represents input voltage. In XY mode, CH1 drives horizontal deflection and CH2 drives vertical deflection.

V = (vertical divisions) × (VOLTS/DIV)
T = (horizontal divisions) × (TIME/DIV),    f = 1/T
φ = 2π Δt/T

Required calibration state

The handout explicitly requires the sweep calibration and both vertical fine-gain controls to remain in their CAL positions, while the X10 horizontal magnifier must not be engaged.

Why: if a fine-adjust control is uncalibrated, the engraved VOLTS/DIV or TIME/DIV value no longer gives the true scale.

The virtual instrument will let you deliberately disturb calibration so you can see why a measurement becomes wrong.

What to notice

  • Sine motion slows near the turning points.
  • Triangle motion has nearly constant speed in each direction.
  • Square input makes the spot jump between two positions.
  • The same signal on X and Y produces a straight diagonal trace.

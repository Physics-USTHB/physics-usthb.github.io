Goniometer

The instrument combines a collimator, prism table and reticle telescope. Readings use the main 0–360° circle plus a 0–30′ micrometer. The slit, reticle and incident-beam reference must be adjusted before the prism is measured.

A = |θ₂−θ₁| / 2
n(λ)=sin[(A+Dₘ)/2] / sin(A/2)

USTHB sequence

  1. Fine slit and reticle adjustment; record θ₀.
  2. Reflection from both prism faces → θ₁, θ₂ → A.
  3. Green Hg line: map D=f(i), determine i₀ and Dₘ.
  4. For all Hg lines, measure Dₘ, calculate n(λ), fit Cauchy's law.
  5. Use Dₘ(λ) as calibration for unknown lamp lines.

USTHB model eye

The physical eye is represented by a convergent lens and a fixed retina. In the scaled bench model, the relaxed eye uses f₃ = 10 cm with the retina 10 cm behind it. The near point of a standard eye is δ = 25 cm.

Magnifier and microscope

The magnifier is represented by a convergent lens of f₂ = 20 cm. For the microscope model the manual uses:

  • objective f₁ = 10 cm,
  • ocular f₂ = 20 cm,
  • eye lens f₃ = 10 cm,
  • optical interval Δ = 80 cm.

Physical interpretation

The objective creates a real, enlarged, inverted intermediate image. The ocular then acts as a magnifier; with the intermediate image in its front focal plane, the emerging rays are parallel and the relaxed model eye forms a sharp retinal image.

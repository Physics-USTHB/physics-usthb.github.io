Part I — geometrical construction with pins

The USTHB sheet first asks you to verify rectilinear propagation by hiding pin A behind successive pins B, C and D for several eye positions. The same sighting method is then used with a plane mirror to verify reflection, reversibility, the virtual image A′ and the mirror field.

i = r     and     A′ = mirror-symmetric point of A

Part II — graduated optical disk

A narrow parallel beam is sent onto a plane mirror, then onto the flat face of a plexiglas half-cylinder. The prescribed incidence angles are 0°, 10°, 20°, 30°, 40°, 50°, 60°, 70°, 80° and 85°.

n = sin i / sin r′

Plot sin r′ versus sin i, then r′(i) and D(i), with D=i−r′.

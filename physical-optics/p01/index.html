Polariser and analyser

For linearly polarised light incident on an analyser at relative angle θ, the transmitted intensity follows Malus' law.

I(θ)=I₀ cos²θ

Quarter-wave plate

A λ/4 plate introduces a π/2 phase delay between its principal axes. At a 45° input orientation, an ideal linearly polarised beam becomes circularly polarised.

Laser → P → λ/4 → A → detector

Physical / Wave Optics Laboratory Practicals

The six digital practical pages reorganise the experiments and analyses contained in the published USTHB L3 Physical Optics handbook: polarisation, Young/Fresnel interference, thin-film/Newton-ring concepts, Michelson interferometry, aperture diffraction and transmission gratings.

USTHB handbook coverage

The Faculty handbook groups the work under Polarisation, Light Interference and Light Diffraction, with multiple manipulations inside each chapter. This website splits that material into six navigable digital practicals; the numbering here is therefore a web-course organisation, not a claim that the handbook itself contains exactly six numbered laboratory sessions.

Diffraction by Slits

Measure Fraunhofer slit patterns and recover aperture dimensions from minima and fringe spacings.

USTHB curriculum topicmeasurement-firstwave-optics analysis

Fraunhofer slit diffraction

For a single slit of width a, minima satisfy a sinθ=mλ. For small angles on a screen at distance D, the first minimum is approximately x₁≈λD/a.

I(θ)=I₀ [sinβ/β]²,   β=πa sinθ/λ

Double slit

A two-slit pattern combines a single-slit diffraction envelope with interference fringes. Measuring both scales separates slit width a from slit spacing d.

Fraunhofer diffraction bench

Locate the first dark minimum with the cursor. In double-slit mode, also measure the fine fringe spacing.

Young & Fresnel Interference

Curriculum-aligned comparison of Young apertures, Fresnel mirrors and a Fresnel biprism through direct fringe-spacing measurements.

Division of wavefront

The published curriculum groups three ways of creating two coherent secondary sources: Young apertures, Fresnel mirrors and a Fresnel biprism. For a small-angle interference pattern:

i = λD/a

Here a is the effective separation of the coherent secondary sources and D is the propagation distance to the observation screen.

Measurement method

Do not infer wavelength from a single pair of adjacent fringes. Align the overlap region, place two screen cursors on bright fringes separated by 10 interfringes, read the millimetre scale, and calculate λ. Repeat with changed geometry for each device.

λ = (10i/10)·a/D

Interference bench

Centre the overlap region, place both cursors on bright fringes exactly ten spacings apart, then calculate λ.

Diffraction Gratings

Use symmetric spectral-order measurements to determine grating density and unknown wavelengths.

USTHB curriculum topicmeasurement-firstwave-optics analysis

Grating equation

For normal incidence, spectral maxima satisfy d sinθ=mλ. A spectrometer/grating measurement can determine the grating spacing or an unknown wavelength.

d sinθ = m λ

Symmetric angular measurement

Measure the same order on the left and right of the zero-order beam. Half the angular separation reduces zero-reference error.

Diffraction-grating spectrometer

Centre the telescope on the selected spectral maximum and record both symmetric sides.

Newton Rings

Measure dark-ring diameters and use the D²–order relation to determine the wavelength or lens curvature.

USTHB curriculum topicmeasurement-firstwave-optics analysis

Newton-ring geometry

A plano-convex lens resting on a plane glass plate creates a thin air film. In reflected monochromatic light the dark-ring diameters obey:

Dₘ² = 4 m λ R

Measurement method

Focus the travelling microscope on the ring plane. Measure left and right positions for several dark rings; use the diameter rather than a radius to reduce centring error. Plot Dₘ² against order m.

Newton-ring microscope

Focus on the ring plane, then measure both sides of each selected dark ring.

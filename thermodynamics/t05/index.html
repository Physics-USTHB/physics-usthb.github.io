Scope transparency

This page is a reviewed educational extension, not a claim that its numeric sequence reproduces an unavailable official handout. It uses standard undergraduate Stirling-engine measurements and keeps all performance calculations in the student workflow.

Energy and power

Pth = UI
Pshaft = 2π(N/60)τ
Pind = WcycleN/60

The area enclosed by the indicated p–V loop is the work delivered by the gas per cycle.

Efficiency bounds

ηshaft = Pshaft/Pth
ηCarnot = 1 − Tc/Th

Temperatures in the Carnot expression must be in kelvin. The real shaft efficiency must remain below the reversible temperature-limit value.

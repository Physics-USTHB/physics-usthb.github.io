Ideal-Gas Laws & Absolute Zero

Use the instrumented gas-law syringe to measure P, V and T, correct the dead volume, verify PV/T, then determine absolute zero by linear regression.

Instrumented syringe

The official apparatus combines an absolute-pressure sensor and a thermistor in a gas-law syringe. DataStudio is configured at 20 Hz for the gas-law experiment.

Absolute-zero apparatus

A fixed gas bulb with pressure and temperature sensors is immersed in hot water, then cooled in ~10°C steps by adding ice. DataStudio uses 10 Hz acquisition; θ(P) is extrapolated to P=0.

Absolute-zero regression

For an ideal gas at fixed n and V, pressure is proportional to absolute temperature. With Celsius temperature, the extrapolated θ-intercept at P=0 estimates absolute zero.

θ = aP + b,   b ≈ −273.15°C

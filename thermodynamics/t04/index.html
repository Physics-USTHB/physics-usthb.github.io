Ideal-Gas Laws & Absolute Zero

Experimental verification of Boyle–Mariotte behaviour and constant-volume pressure thermometry, followed by an extrapolation to absolute zero.

P–V isothermP–T isochorelinear regressionabsolute-zero extrapolation

Boyle–Mariotte law

For a fixed amount of gas at constant temperature, slowly change the trapped volume and allow the gas to re-equilibrate before reading the pressure.

PV = nRT = constant    (T constant)

A plot of P versus 1/V should be linear. Use absolute pressure throughout.

Pressure thermometer

Seal a fixed volume of gas and vary its bath temperature. At constant volume the ideal-gas law predicts a linear pressure–temperature relation.

P = nR(T°C−T0)/V

Fit P=a+bT°C; the extrapolated pressure-zero temperature is T0=−a/b.

Thermal Expansion & the Water-Density Anomaly

USTHB two-part practical: linear expansion of copper/aluminium and experimental demonstration of water's anomalous density near 4°C.

Cu & Al dilatometerleast-squares fit0.5°C water scandensity maximum

Part 1 — copper and aluminium

ΔL = αL₀ΔT

Measure L₀ and T₀ for each rod, zero the dilatometer, circulate thermostated water through the hollow rod, and record several pairs (ΔT, ΔL). Fit ΔL=f(ΔT) by least squares; the slope is αL₀.

Part 2 — anomaly of water

The water volume is inferred from the height h in a narrow capillary while the apparatus is cooled in an ice/salt bath and stirred. Record h every 0.5°C until the temperature falls below about 0.5°C.

ρ(θ)/ρ(0) = [V₀+A h(0)] / [V₀(1+3αgθ)+A h(θ)]

V₀=325 mL, capillary diameter d=1.5 mm, glass linear coefficient αg=3.3×10⁻⁶ K⁻¹.

Thermal Expansion

Measure the linear expansion coefficients of copper and aluminium, then demonstrate the anomalous thermal behaviour of water from 0 to 15°C.

USTHB protocolmeasurement-firstenergy balance & uncertainty

Linear expansion

ΔL = α L₀ ΔT

The USTHB rig circulates thermostated water through a copper or aluminium rod. The dilatometer must be brought into contact and zeroed before heating.

Water anomaly

Between 0 and 4°C water contracts when heated, reaching maximum density near 4°C. The experiment uses a 325 ml vessel, a 1.5 mm riser column and a crushed-ice/salt bath with magnetic stirring.

Thermal-expansion apparatus

Check the hoses and water reservoir, circulate water for 10 s, then zero the dilatometer.

Linear Thermal Expansion

Measure the elongation of heated metal rods with a dial gauge, plot ΔL against ΔT, and determine the linear expansion coefficient from the experimental slope.

heated roddial gaugeΔL vs ΔTcoefficient α

Method

  1. Select a rod and let it reach the initial laboratory temperature.
  2. Set the dial gauge to zero at the reference temperature.
  3. Heat the rod to successive stable temperatures without changing the mechanical contact.
  4. Read the thermometer and dial gauge at thermal equilibrium.
  5. Plot ΔL versus ΔT and determine α from the slope.

Relation

ΔL = α L₀ ΔT

The virtual rods have a measured reference length L₀ = 600.0 mm. The dial gauge reads 0.01 mm divisions. The numerical material constants remain hidden until the student has completed the slope analysis.

A physical setup also involves contact pressure, backlash, imperfect thermal uniformity and heat loss; the simulator reproduces their measurement consequences with small repeatable offsets.

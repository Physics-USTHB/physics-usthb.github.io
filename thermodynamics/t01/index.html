Latent Heats of Water

Determine the latent heat of vaporisation from steam condensation and the latent heat of fusion from ice calorimetry, following the USTHB two-part procedure.

USTHB protocolmeasurement-firstenergy balance & uncertainty

Two calorimetric balances

Part 1 sends steam through an expansion vessel into cold water in a calorimeter. Part 2 adds dry ice cubes to warm water. Both use ΣQ=0 for an approximately thermally isolated system.

Q=m c ΔT    and    Q=mL

USTHB operating points

  • Steam: begin with water about 10°C below room temperature and stop near the original room temperature.
  • Ice: begin about 15°C above room temperature and stop about 15°C below it.
  • Stir continuously while reading the thermometer.
  • Masses are obtained by weighing the calorimeter before and after.

THERMODYNAMICS · LABORATORY PRACTICAL 1

Calorimetry & Latent Heats

Calibrate the calorimeter, then determine the latent heat of fusion of ice and the latent heat of vaporisation from mass and equilibrium-temperature measurements.

calorimeter calibrationfusion of icesteam condensationenergy balance

Core method

  1. Determine the calorimeter heat capacity by mixing measured quantities of cold and hot water.
  2. Use the calibrated calorimeter in a warm-water/ice mixture and apply the complete energy balance.
  3. Use a steam-condensation mixture to determine the latent heat of vaporisation.
  4. Compare the independently measured values with accepted physical values only after the experimental calculations are complete.

Energy accounting

Use the same sign convention throughout: heat lost by the initially hotter body equals heat gained by the initially colder body plus any phase-change energy.

Q = mcΔT    and    Qphase = mL

The virtual balance reads to 0.1 g and the thermometer to 0.1 °C. Small repeatable measurement offsets are part of the simulated apparatus.

Safety and handling

In a physical laboratory, hot water and steam can burn. The virtual practical reproduces the measurement sequence and thermal balances, not the physical handling risk. In the real laboratory, dry the ice surface before weighing and do not direct steam toward hands or faces.

Electrical Calorimetry & Specific Heat

Use measured electrical energy and temperature rise to calibrate a calorimeter, then determine the specific heat capacities of two unknown solid specimens.

Joule heatingheat capacityspecific heatenergy balance

Electrical method

A heater immersed in water delivers electrical energy (E=UIt). With the calorimeter alone, the measured temperature rise determines its heat capacity. Repeating the heating with a known mass of solid immersed in the same water adds the specimen heat capacity.

UI t = (mwcw + Ccal + mscs)ΔT

Measurement practice

  1. Read voltage and current under load, not only nominal supply settings.
  2. Start timing when the heater is energised.
  3. Stir gently so the thermometer represents the bulk temperature.
  4. Choose heating time so ΔT is large enough to resolve but not excessive.
  5. Use the calibrated calorimeter value for both specimens.

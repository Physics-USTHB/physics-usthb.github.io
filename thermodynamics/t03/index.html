Water Equivalent of a Calorimeter

USTHB electrical calorimetry practical using a corrected temperature rise obtained from pre-heating and post-heating thermal-drift extrapolations.

Joule heatingthree-stage temperature recordthermal-loss correctionwater equivalent W

Purpose

The calorimeter itself absorbs heat. Its heat capacity is expressed as an equivalent mass W of water.

QJoule = U²τ/R = (m+W)cwater ΔTcorr
W = U²τ/(R c ΔTcorr) − m

The corrected rise is not simply the highest temperature minus the initial temperature: heat exchange with the room must be estimated from the temperature drift before and after heating.

Measurement sequence

  1. Measure empty and water-filled calorimeter masses, room temperature, resistance R and heater voltage U.
  2. With the heater off, record one temperature each minute for 15 minutes.
  3. Switch the heater on for 10 minutes while stirring continuously and continue the minute-by-minute record.
  4. Switch the heater off and record the cooling drift for another 15 minutes.
  5. Extrapolate the pre- and post-heating drift lines to the midpoint of the heating interval and use their separation as ΔTcorr.

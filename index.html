L1 Electricity laboratory

Five practicals. One coherent experimental sequence.

Each module follows the corresponding USTHB practical workflow where source material is available, while clearly separating digital extensions from the core laboratory protocol.

TP1 · E01Electrical Measurements & Resistance

Analog ranges, class uncertainty, potentiometric source, short and long derivation.

Measure · uncertainty · resistance → TP2 · E02Rheographic Tank

Equipotentials, field reconstruction, symmetry, edge effects and conducting cylinder.

Map · field lines · potential → TP3 · E03I–V Characteristics

Resistor and lamp characteristics with series and parallel graphical verification.

Acquire · graph · verify → TP4 · E04The Oscilloscope

XY mode, calibrated sweep, triggering, frequency, phase and Lissajous figures.

Operate · synchronise · measure → TP5 · E05RC & RL Transients

Time constants, graphical methods and determination of unknown R, C and L.

Observe · estimate τ · infer →
Scope: these modules reproduce the intellectual and measurement workflow of the practicals, but they do not claim to replace every physical bench skill such as real cable routing, probe pressure, equipment faults, water handling, component tolerances, or instructor sign-off.
Academic standard

Designed as university coursework, not gamified revision.

Every experiment is built around the logic of experimental physics: define a model, choose a measurement strategy, understand the instrument, acquire data, quantify uncertainty, test the model, and communicate a defensible conclusion.

Inspect the complete Electricity suite ↗
01

Measurement first

Finite resolution, internal resistance, calibration offsets, instrument loading, and noise are part of the experiment.

02

Analysis, not animation

Students collect their own data, inspect residuals, evaluate fit statistics, and compare measurements with theory.

03

Scientific communication

Each laboratory ends with an English-language report requiring interpretation, not just automatically generated numbers.

β and γ Radiation in Aluminium

Curriculum-aligned aluminium-absorber study using raw counts, measured background, beta practical range and gamma exponential attenuation.

USTHB curriculum topicraw countingβ practical rangeγ attenuation

Two attenuation behaviours

For a representative ~662 keV gamma source, aluminium attenuation is approximately exponential in a fixed narrow-beam geometry (μ ≈ 0.020 mm⁻¹). Beta particles instead show a finite-range-like falloff. Actual coefficients depend on source energy and geometry.

Iγ(x)=I₀e−μx

For beta radiation, estimate a practical range from the thickness where the background-corrected rate becomes only a small fraction of the unabsorbed rate.

Counting procedure

Measure the detector background first. Then keep source–absorber–detector geometry and counting time controlled while increasing aluminium thickness for each radiation.

Inet=N/t−Ibg

Use raw integer counts and propagate the measured background into all reductions.

Source → aluminium → detector

Measure background first, then obtain x=0 and a well-spaced thickness series for both β and γ; reach 30–40 mm for the weaker gamma attenuation.

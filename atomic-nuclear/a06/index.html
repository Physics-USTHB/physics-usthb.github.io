Why vacuum?

Alpha particles lose energy rapidly in matter and have a short range in air. High-resolution alpha spectroscopy therefore places the source and silicon detector in a low-pressure chamber.

The public USTHB programme names Alpha particle spectroscopy as Practical 6; this page is a reviewed digital reconstruction rather than an exact apparatus claim.

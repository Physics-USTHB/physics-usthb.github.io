Franck–Hertz Experiment

Inelastic collisions

Electrons accelerated through mercury vapour lose a nearly fixed energy when they excite Hg atoms. Repeated inelastic collisions generate periodic maxima/minima in collector current as the accelerating voltage increases.

ΔU ≈ Eexc/e

Spectral connection

The first excitation energy can be expressed as an equivalent photon wavelength:

Eexc=hc/λ

Use several current maxima rather than one spacing; repeat with a second retarding-potential setting to test whether the spacing remains unchanged.

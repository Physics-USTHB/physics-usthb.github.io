Electron-spin resonance

For an unpaired electron in magnetic field B, the Zeeman splitting matches the applied RF quantum at resonance.

h f = g μB Bres

Measurement

At several RF frequencies, sweep the magnetic field through resonance and record Bres. The slope f(B) yields the spectroscopic g-factor.

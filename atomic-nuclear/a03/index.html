Electron Spin Resonance

Curriculum-aligned ESR reconstruction using RF-frequency and magnetic-field measurements to determine the electron spectroscopic g-factor.

USTHB curriculum topicdigital reconstructionresonance searchg-factor fit

Zeeman resonance

For an unpaired electron in a magnetic field, resonance occurs when the Zeeman splitting equals the applied RF photon energy.

hf = g μBBres

Measure the field at maximum absorption for several RF frequencies. A straight-line fit of f versus B gives the slope gμB/h.

Measurement discipline

The public USTHB programme gives the ESR topic but not a detailed equipment sheet, so the apparatus is a reviewed digital reconstruction. Calibrate the field coils, sweep B slowly, and record the field reading yourself at each resonance maximum.

g = (h/μB)·df/dB

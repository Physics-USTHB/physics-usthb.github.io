Measurement discipline

  1. Allow the He–Ne laser to warm up.
  2. Zero the power meter before the scan.
  3. Rotate the analyser through 0–180° and record the meter reading yourself.
  4. Use the angular scan to obtain the polarisation axis, extrema and degree of linear polarisation.
DoLP=(Pmax−Pmin)/(Pmax+Pmin)

Theoretical background

A He–Ne laser emits a highly directional beam whose electric field is predominantly linearly polarised. An analyser transmits the field component along its own axis, so an ideal linearly polarised beam follows Malus' law: P(θ)=P₀cos²(θ−θ₀).

Real beams and detectors can leave a non-zero minimum, modelled here by Pmin. The degree of linear polarisation DoLP=(Pmax−Pmin)/(Pmax+Pmin) is therefore obtained from the measured extrema, while θ₀ gives the polarisation-axis orientation.

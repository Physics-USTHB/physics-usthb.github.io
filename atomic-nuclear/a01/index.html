He–Ne laser polarisation

The official USTHB title asks for correlation between laser power and polarisation. The reconstruction uses a rotating analyser and power meter to determine the laser's polarisation axis and extinction behaviour.

P(θ)=P₀ cos²(θ−θ₀)+Pmin

Measurement sequence

  1. Warm the laser and zero the power meter.
  2. Rotate analyser 0–180°.
  3. Record power at regular angles.
  4. Fit the Malus dependence and infer θ₀.
  5. Compare maximum/minimum power and degree of linear polarisation.
